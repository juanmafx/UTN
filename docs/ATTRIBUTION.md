## Code of unknown origin

The original binary conversion and card-dealing exercises contain tutorial-style explanations and may have been adapted
from public learning resources. The source could not be identified. The modern implementations in `NumberBases` and
`Shuffling` were rewritten, and both algorithms are standard.

## Algorithms

- Fisher-Yates shuffle: Ronald Fisher and Frank Yates, later popularized by Richard Durstenfeld and Donald Knuth.
- Triangle area arrangement: William Kahan.
- Heron's formula: Hero of Alexandria.
- Pearson's chi-squared test: Karl Pearson.
